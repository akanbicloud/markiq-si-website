How an event card works

Before, during and after — in one card.

Instead of a flood of messages, each big release gets one card that updates itself as the story unfolds.

15 min before

The heads-up

Forecast, previous figure, and what would count as a surprise — so you're ready, not rushed.

At release

The number lands

Actual versus forecast, the surprise level, and which way it went — confirmed by the official source.

15 and 60 min after

The reaction

Currency strength before and after, and how the markets that matter actually moved.

Context

The history

What usually happened after similar releases, with honest sample sizes — never a buy or sell call.
