How inflation data moves currencies

Video lesson · 12 min · coming soon

Key points

  • CPI tracks the change in prices that households pay for everyday goods and services.
  • Markets react to the surprise — the gap between the actual number and the forecast — more than to the number itself.
  • Hotter-than-expected inflation makes higher interest rates more likely, which often (not always) strengthens the currency.
  • Cooler-than-expected inflation does the opposite. Always check how big the surprise was before reacting.
